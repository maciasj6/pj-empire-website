The work

Four light-industrial buildings rising on Airport Road in Oceanside — and on this one, the roof is the job. PJ Empire furnishes and installs the panelized roof structures over the masonry shells: glulam and sawn framing, sheathing, and the hardware that ties it all together.

Each building also carries a mezzanine floor package — joists and sheathing set into the shell, ready for the trades that follow. Four buildings, one crew rhythm, repeated clean.

Scope

  • Panelized roof structures
  • Roof sheathing
  • Mezzanine floor joists & sheathing
  • Glulam & sawn lumber package
  • Hardware & connections
  • Buildings A–D
