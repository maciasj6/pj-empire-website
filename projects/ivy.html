The work

A full city block of senior living rising on Baldwin Avenue — 104,831 square feet of Type III wood framing across three stories. PJ Empire self-performs the complete rough carpentry package: every wall line, every floor, every piece of hardware, set by our own crews.

On a footprint this large, framing pace sets the project pace. Our crews run the deck in continuous sequence — layout, walls, shear, floor package — so the trades behind us are never waiting on wood.

Scope

  • All wood wall framing
  • Floor joists & sheathing
  • Shear panel & hardware
  • Tie-down system
  • Stairs
  • Hoisting & coordination
